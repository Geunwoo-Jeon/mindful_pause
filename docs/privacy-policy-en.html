Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 26, 2025

🔒 Key Summary
"Pause, Reflect" app does not collect or transmit any personal information externally.
All data is stored only on your device and is never shared with servers or third parties.

1. Information Collection and Use

1.1 Information We Store

This app stores the following information only on your device:

  • Goals: Your 5-year, 1-year, and 3-month goals
  • Answer Records: Your responses to self-reflection questions and timestamps
  • App Settings: Reminder interval, pause settings, etc.

1.2 Data Storage Method

All data is stored using Android's Room Database exclusively on your device.

  • ✅ Stored only in device internal storage
  • ✅ No server transmission
  • ✅ No cloud synchronization
  • ✅ No third-party sharing

2. App Permissions and Their Purpose

This app requests the following permissions to provide its features:

2.1 Required Permissions

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to display self-reflection question popups
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to show background service status notifications
  • Accessibility Service: Used to detect app usage and show questions at appropriate times. Does not collect any text input or personal information—only detects app switch events.
  • Usage Stats (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used to display today's screen time. Statistics are calculated on-device only and never transmitted externally.

2.2 Optional Permissions

  • Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to automatically restart the service after device reboot (can be disabled by user)

3. Third-Party Information Sharing

This app does not share any personal information with third parties.

  • ❌ No advertising platforms
  • ❌ No analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • ❌ No server connections
  • ❌ No social media integrations

4. Data Retention and Deletion

4.1 Retention Period

All data is retained on your device until you uninstall the app.

4.2 Deletion Methods

You can completely delete your data using either method:

  • Method 1: Uninstall the app (Device Settings > Apps > Pause, Reflect > Uninstall)
  • Method 2: Clear app data (Device Settings > Apps > Pause, Reflect > Storage > Clear Data)

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover that information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

6. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in laws, policies, or app features. Significant changes will be announced through app update notes.
